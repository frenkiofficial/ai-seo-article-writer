# AI SEO Article Writer & Analyzer — Demo

An interactive demo of an AI agent service for creators and small businesses, by **Aqua Ter**.

## What it does

1. **Writes from your brief** — type a topic and a main keyword, pick a length and a tone, and the writer drafts a structured article: SEO title, meta description, introduction, sections with subheadings, a checklist or tips list, FAQ and conclusion.
2. **Scores it honestly** — the analyzer calculates a 0–100 SEO score from seven real checks on the draft itself: article length, keyword density, readability (Flesch reading ease), SEO title, meta description, keyword in subheadings, and keyword in the first 100 words.
3. **Tells you what to fix** — every suggestion is computed from your article with concrete numbers ("density is 0.4% — add the keyword about 6 more times", "the title is 72 characters — aim for 40–60"), not generic advice.
4. **Built to use** — highlight the keyword in the draft, write a new draft with one click, copy the text or HTML, or download the article as Markdown.

Two sample topics are built in: indoor plants and pour over coffee. Every input changes the output — try your own topic and watch the score move.

## This demo

`index.html` + `styles.css` + `app.js` + `data.js` — a static page, no backend, no tracking. Article drafts are assembled from template banks and scored live in the browser.

**Sample data:** the articles, scores and suggestions in this demo are generated examples for demonstration only — not guarantees of search rankings. In a real run, the writer uses the client's brand voice, research and keyword data.
