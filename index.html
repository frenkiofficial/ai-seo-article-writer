      How the score works

      The overall score is a weighted average of seven checks: article length (20%), keyword density (20%), readability (20%), SEO title (10%), meta description (10%), keyword in subheadings (10%) and keyword in the first 100 words (10%).

      • Length — inside the target range for the chosen length (short 400–600, medium 700–1,000, long 1,050–1,400 words).
      • Keyword density — 1.0–2.0% of all words. Below that, search engines may miss the topic; above it, the text reads as stuffed.
      • Readability — Flesch reading ease, 60–70 is plain English. Calculated from real sentence and syllable counts.
      • Title & meta — title 40–60 characters with the keyword; meta description 120–160 characters.
